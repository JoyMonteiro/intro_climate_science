About the data

Radiation: CERES EBAF Edition 4.2.1 (NASA Langley), monthly means from January 2010 to January 2025. Here the 1° grid is averaged onto a 2° grid, weighting each cell by its area. DOI 10.5067/TERRA-AQUA-NOAA20/CERES/EBAF_L3B004.2.1.

Sign convention: positive means energy going into the system named: into the Earth at the top of the atmosphere, into the surface at the surface, and into the atmosphere for the column.

Anomalies are differences from the 2010–2024 average of the same calendar months.

Global means are area-weighted over the whole Earth. For the two albedos they are the global reflected sunlight divided by the global incoming sunlight, so polar-night cells, where an albedo has no value, still count through their fluxes.

El Niño and La Niña: the NOAA CPC Niño-3.4 sea-surface temperature anomaly (ERSSTv5, 1991–2020 base). Shading marks spells of at least five months when its 3-month mean is at or above +0.5 °C (El Niño) or at or below −0.5 °C (La Niña).
